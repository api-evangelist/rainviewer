The beginning of 2025 was marked by tragedy, with devastating fires raging across Los Angeles, California. The Pacific Palisades Fire and Eaton Fire were the most destructive ones. More than 100 fires ravaged the upscale neighborhood, destroying over 12,000 structures and the homes of several prominent celebrities.
