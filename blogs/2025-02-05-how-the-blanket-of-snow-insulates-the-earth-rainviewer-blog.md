When you think of snow, you might imagine a cold, white layer that covers everything in its path. But despite its chilly appearance, snow acts as a protective blanket that insulates and preserves life beneath its surface. From safeguarding plants and animals to maintaining soil health, the science behind snow’s insulating properties is fascinating.
