Picnics conjure images of sunny skies, soft breezes, and comfortably warm temperatures - a day where everything aligns just right for outdoor enjoyment. But is this idyllic vision of perfect picnic weather something real, or is it just a myth? It turns out that the “perfect weather” is deeply subjective, and the factors that shape our perception of an ideal day vary from person to person.
