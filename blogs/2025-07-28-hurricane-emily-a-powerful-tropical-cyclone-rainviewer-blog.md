In a season that was already buzzing with activity, Hurricane Emily stood out early. Having become the earliest Atlantic hurricane on record to reach Category 5 strength, Emily made landfall twice, in the Caribbean and on Mexico’s Gulf Coast. It also showed clear radar and satellite signs like sharp eyewalls and intense convection bursts. More than just powerful, Emily became a standout case study in how fast storms can evolve across the Atlantic.
