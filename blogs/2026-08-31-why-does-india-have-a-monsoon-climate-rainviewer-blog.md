TL;DR: India’s monsoon comes from four things working together: land that heats faster than the ocean, a rain belt that shifts north, heating over the Tibetan Plateau, and mountains that decide where the rain lands. A monsoon is a seasonal reversal of prevailing winds that creates a distinct wet season. The southwest monsoon reaches Kerala in the first week of June and covers the whole country by early to mid July.
