The year 2011 witnessed one of Thailand’s worst natural disasters - a series of floods that ravaged the country, leaving a trail of destruction and displacing millions. This blog post delves into the 2011 Thailand floods chronology. We will trace their path from the initial monsoon rains to the peak inundation and the long road to recovery. Thailand Monsoon Floods 2011 Thailand’s climate is heavily influenced by monsoons, with a rainy season typically lasting from May to October. While flooding is common for this region, the early months of 2011 hinted at an impending disaster. Unusually heavy rainfall began in May, raising concerns among meteorologists. Then came Tropical Storm Nock-ten.
