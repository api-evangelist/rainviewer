Forecasting the weather isn’t just a science - it’s a skill. And like any skill, it sharpens with time, curiosity, and the occasional wrong call. From misread radar echoes to overconfidence in a single model run, even seasoned enthusiasts hit bumps along the forecasting road. At RainViewer, we believe every misstep is a chance to grow. So we’ve gathered five of the most common pitfalls that trip up amateur meteorologists and radar geeks alike.
