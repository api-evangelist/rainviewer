TL;DR: MetMalaysia thunderstorm warnings follow a tiered structure, and each tier carries its own recommended action. Levels run from ordinary thunderstorms to severe storms with damaging wind and flash-flood risk Each level is tied to specific advice, not just to the severity of the phenomenon Tropical thunderstorms form by convection, which makes them intensely local The same area can hold different levels for different hazards at the same time A warning covers a whole district, not the single location in the storm’s path A MetMalaysia thunderstorm warning turns up on phones across the count
