Modern meteorology owes much of its progress to radar technology. Radar systems provide critical real-time insights into weather conditions, helping forecasters predict severe weather and issue timely warnings. Among the many tools in a meteorologist’s arsenal are radar signatures - unique patterns observed in radar imagery , which indicate extreme weather phenomena.
