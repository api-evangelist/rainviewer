Weather is full of surprises, but few phenomena can be as counterintuitive as temperature inversions. Instead of the air cooling as it rises, which is the usual pattern in the atmosphere, temperature inversions flip the script. Warmer air sits atop cooler air, creating unique and sometimes extreme weather conditions.
