TL;DR: A gota fria, which AEMET calls a DANA, is a pocket of cold air aloft that in autumn meets a Mediterranean still holding summer heat, and it can drop more than 700 l/m2 in one day. DANA stands for isolated depression at high levels. Gota fria is the popular name for the same phenomenon.
