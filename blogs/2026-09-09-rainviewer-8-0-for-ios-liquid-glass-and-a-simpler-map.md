RainViewer 8.0 for iOS is out. On iOS 26 the app adopts Apple’s Liquid Glass design. On every iPhone and iPad, the map screen is simpler: the name of the active layer is written on the map, the player shows one time, playback options live behind one button, and a new fullscreen mode gives the whole screen to the radar.
